HW02 submission video / repeatable 360-degree showcase

Open Assets/Scenes/MIO Showcase.unity and press Play. The 36-second camera sequence loops. P pauses/resumes; R restarts; Space changes the material manually and overrides scheduled style cuts until restart/the next loop. The existing MIO Interactive Study scene remains available for the fixed front-view interaction.

The color scene completes one uninterrupted 360-degree orbit from 2s to 18s. There is a small camera elevation change to reveal volume. Graphite is demonstrated from 20s to 25s, Dream Palette is shown from 25s to 32s, followed by a return to color/front. Vertex animation and stepped outline animation advance throughout.

Videos/MIO/MIO-Turnaround-1080p.mp4 is the 36-second, 1920x1080, 30 fps submission recording. It contains 1080 deterministic Unity-rendered frames and no audio. It is a scene recording, not a transformation of a still image. The physical keyboard is not filmed; scheduled cuts invoke the same material selection used by the interactive scene.

Use HW02 MIO > 11 - Export 36 second 1080p showcase video to reproduce the recording in the Unity Editor. The exporter uses the built-in UnityEditor.Media.MediaEncoder and needs no third-party Unity package. Output is relative to the project root.

Screenshots/MIO/Showcase contains inspection frames and validation. Model side/back forms are the original 3D interpretation of a single front-view concept reference.

Remaining submission work: user-selected BGM, final review and PR.
