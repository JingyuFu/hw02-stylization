HW02 - Task 2: Interesting Shaders

Open Assets/Scenes/MIO Shader Study.unity and press Play.

MIO Toon.shadergraph derives from the user's Lab 03 shader. It combines main/additional lights, a three-color palette and a custom seamless hatching texture sampled in UV0 with Shadow Scale. SoftRim adds a view-dependent, lighting-weighted rim; use Rim Strength = 0 to compare. Rim Color, Start and Softness control its appearance.

MIO Living Filaments.shadergraph duplicates that improved graph and adds FilamentWave in the vertex stage (task 2 special shader, option 2). Smoothstep pins the roots; sine/cosine waves displace vertices along all three axes. UV1 carries curve distance and its gradient, while UV0 remains the surface UV. The vertex normal/tangent are transformed with the deformation Jacobian. End caps share ring data and bounds cover the full amplitude range. Only the 12 long filaments use this shader; the lens, pearl and throat strands remain static.

Materials 01-06: Rim Strength / Rim Color / Rim Start / Rim Softness.
Materials 09-10: Wave Amplitude / Wave Speed / Wave Count / Wave Phase. Zero amplitude restores the rest shape; zero speed freezes movement. A hidden Preview Time is used for repeatable captures, and is saved at -1 for normal Time-node animation.

Screenshots/MIO/Shaders includes on/off rim renders, two animation times and GPU validation results.
